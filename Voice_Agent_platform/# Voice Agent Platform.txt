# Voice Agent Platform

## Platform Used: Retell AI

**Retell AI** was used to build and test the Home Credit Loan Against Property (LAP) voice agent. It enables AI-powered voice conversations with customers and supports testing conversational flows for preliminary loan eligibility screening.

## Purpose

The voice agent is designed to contact customers, explain the Home Credit LAP offer, collect eligibility information, identify disqualifying conditions, and route eligible customers to a senior loan expert.

## Key Capabilities Used

- **AI Voice Conversations:** Conducts natural, interactive conversations with customers.
- **Customer Verification:** Verifies that the agent is speaking to the intended customer.
- **Availability Check:** Determines whether the customer can talk and handles callback requests.
- **Eligibility Screening:** Collects property, ownership, document, loan amount, occupation, income, market value, and tenure details.
- **Existing Loan Detection:** Identifies existing property loans, EMI reduction requests, and loan-transfer cases for specialist handling.
- **Eligibility Decisions:** Applies the defined screening rules and disqualifies customers when mandatory criteria fail.
- **Expert Handoff:** Routes eligible customers to a senior loan expert for further assessment.
- **Scenario Testing:** Tests eligible cases, disqualifications, ambiguous responses, contradictory answers, and out-of-order information.

## Eligibility Rules Implemented

1. Accepted property types: residential, commercial, and industrial.
2. Agricultural property is not eligible.
3. Sole and joint property ownership are accepted.
4. Original property documents must be available for verification.
5. Maximum requested loan amount: ₹75 lakh.
6. Salaried and self-employed customers are accepted.
7. Income must be received through a bank; cash-only income is disqualified.
8. Accepted loan tenure: 3 to 15 years.
9. Existing loan, EMI reduction, or loan-transfer cases are identified for specialist handling.

## Testing and Integration

The voice agent was tested using multiple customer scenarios. Audio recordings and transcripts were maintained as test evidence.

An n8n automation workflow named **Home Credit LAP - Retell Results** processes call-result events, extracts relevant information, evaluates transcripts, and records results in Google Sheets.

## Project Limitations

- The voice agent performs preliminary eligibility screening, not final loan approval.
- Speech recognition errors and ambiguous customer responses may require manual review.
- Final eligibility, loan terms, and approval are subject to assessment by the authorized loan expert.
- API credentials, webhook secrets, and customer-sensitive information must not be included in the public repository.

## Outcome

The project demonstrates the use of conversational AI and workflow automation to streamline preliminary LAP eligibility screening, identify important customer cases, and maintain structured test records.
